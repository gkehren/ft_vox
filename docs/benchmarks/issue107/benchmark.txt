Issue #107 - sectioned meshes for localized remeshing
======================================================

Change
------
The logical chunk stays 16x256x16; its full-quality render mesh is now one
payload per vertical 16x16x16 section (16 sections, same tiling as the #105
occupancy metadata). Edits mark only the affected sections (own section,
Y-boundary neighbors, the horizontal neighbor's y/16 section for border
mirrors, plus a conservative light range: emissive +-14, skylight column
down to its first blocker). Lighting is recomputed chunk-wide per build (the
issue's sanctioned option) so rebuilt sections sample exactly the field a
whole build would produce - no seams at 16-block boundaries; regional
lighting is future work. Each stream (opaque/water) remains ONE vertex/index
buffer pair per chunk with back-to-back per-section slots, keeping the
renderer at one bind + drawIndexed per chunk; uploads re-stage only the
dirty slots in three passes (plan + staging reservation before any GPU
mutation, offset-preserving buffer growth, then copy recording + commit).
Design documented in docs/engine-architecture.md ("Sectioned meshes").

Edit benchmark (test_mesh_facekey --edit-bench, median of 5 builds, one
generated chunk with a generated east neighbor, same machine)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
scenario                        | section ms | full ms | sections | staged KiB | full KiB
mid-section surface place       |     0.8092 |  1.5369 |     2/16 |     32.0   |  150.3
y-boundary place (y=64)         |     0.7652 |  1.5076 |     2/16 |     32.0   |  150.3
chunk-border place + mirror     |     0.5517 |  1.6238 |     1/16 |      8.4   |  151.0
burst of 24 edits, one section  |     0.7656 |  1.5041 |     1/16 |     31.9   |  151.1
16 edits spread over 8 sections |     2.2335 |  2.1712 |     8/16 |    168.4   |  168.4

A single edit rebuilds ~2 sections (own + light/boundary neighbor) at ~2-3x
less CPU and ~5-18x fewer staged bytes than a whole-chunk rebuild; a spread
burst costs the same as a full rebuild (chunk-wide lighting dominates),
which is the documented next frontier. Empty sections never reach the
mesher (occupancy metadata) and never allocate GPU slots until they hold
content.

Correctness
-----------
- Partial rebuild == full rebuild: a mask-restricted build reproduces the
  full build's section byte for byte (chunk lifecycle section 20), for
  terrain and synthetic multi-section scenes.
- Dirty contract pinned: mid-section, y%16==0 boundary, emissive radius,
  skylight range (never above the edit), border mirror = exactly y/16.
- #115 metadata-bounds vs forced-full-range equivalence and #106 face-key
  scene contracts still pass (sections compared byte-wise).
- Mesh upload lifecycle reworked for slots: staging-full defers with ZERO
  GPU-state mutation, retry succeeds from the same result, partial staging
  failure keeps the CPU payload attached and the old GPU mesh untouched.
- Multi-worker concurrent builds byte-identical; 20x Release repeats of
  chunk lifecycle + mesh facekey green; ctest Release 19/19; Debug CRT
  unavailable in this environment (loader error), CI covers Debug/ASan/TSan.
