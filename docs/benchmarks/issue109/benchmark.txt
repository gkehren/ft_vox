Issue #109 benchmarks — shared mesh arenas + indirect drawing
==============================================================

Scope: replace per-chunk mesh VMA buffers with 4 shared device-local
arenas (opaque/water x vertex/index, paged, frame-aware retirement) and
submit opaque/water/shadow geometry through vkCmdDrawIndexedIndirect.

Base: origin/main d677d9d (post-#118). Branch: gpu-mesh-arenas at 4266a51
(measured after the multiDrawIndirect enablement; the follow-up
maxDrawIndirectCount batching fix does not touch steady-state batches on
hardware whose limit is UINT32_MAX).
Machine: Windows 11, MSVC Release, Ryzen 7 9800X3D, RTX 4070 Ti.
Protocol: interleaved runs (main, PR, main, PR, ...), median of 4 pairs,
`ft_vox --seed 42 --benchmark 30` (30 s measured, 3 s warmup), view
distance 512, vsync off, validation off.

Notes:
- The "draws.opaque" counter changes meaning with this PR: it now counts
  indirect commands - exactly one per live section, never merged
  (section-local indices are rebased by the command's vertexOffset, so
  merging would redirect indices to another section's vertices). Commands
  are grouped only at submission time by (vertexPage, indexPage) to
  reduce buffer binds. The shadow total is ~3x the opaque count (one
  collection per cascade), water adds its back-to-front list.
- indirect.commands.peak (~13.9k = opaque + cascades + water demand per
  frame, pre-truncation) sits at ~21% of kMaxIndirectCommands (65,536):
  no overflow at view distance 512; dynamic batching stays follow-up.
- Frame/GPU deltas slightly favor the branch and are within run-to-run
  noise. Record carries the CPU cost of building the
  per-pass indirect command lists (+0.05 ms avg): collecting per-section
  commands for opaque, three shadow cascades and the ordered water list
  every frame. This is the documented cost of the CPU-built indirect
  path; the identified follow-ups are per-chunk cached draw descriptors
  (rebuild at upload-commit instead of per pass per frame) and then
  GPU-driven culling/count.
- Physics is suspended during this benchmark; renderer comparison only.

VMA allocation churn (the issue's core target)
----------------------------------------------
mesh.allocations.created over a 30 s streaming run:
  main:   59,848 allocations (4+ per chunk upload, retired per frame)
  arenas: 3 allocations (page creation events only)

Arena metrics (arena.* gauges, 30 s run): pages stay single-digit,
growEvents bounded by steady-state world size; empty pages are released
through the frame-aware retire queue.

 Vulkan validation
------------------
FT_VOX_VALIDATION=1 + Khronos layer (SDK 1.3.290) over the same 30 s
benchmark: 0 sync hazards, 0 object-leak reports (the arena/page retire
ordering is exercised in Engine::~Engine: the retire queue must outlive
chunk/world teardown).
- Validation caught a real spec violation during the review cycle: the
  grouped indirect draws used drawCount > 1 without enabling
  multiDrawIndirect (VUID-vkCmdDrawIndexedIndirect-drawCount-02718,
  ~508k reports/run, tolerated by the NVIDIA driver). Fixed by enabling
  the core feature when advertised, with a drawCount=1 per-command
  fallback otherwise.
- Final runs: indirect 30 s and FT_VOX_DRAW_DIRECT 10 s both report
  zero indirect VUIDs. The two remaining reports (swapchain
  imageFormat-01778, sRGB storage view usage-02275) reproduce on main
  d677d9d and predate this PR.
